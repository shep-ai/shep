Act II · Night

The turn

Ch 2 · The MAYA Rule

Most advanced, yet acceptable

Raymond Loewy's rule: push to the boldest design people will still accept, then stop. Shep's current pitches sit at the two ends. One is too familiar to notice. The other is too big to picture.

Keep: everything they already know

  • Your repo and your GitHub issues
  • The agent you already use, on the subscription you already pay for
  • Ordinary pull requests and your own CI
  • You still hold the merge button

Add: one surprise

It works while you sleep. You wake up to PRs that already pass CI.

shep tonightshep morning

The brand already fits. Shepherds keep watch over their flocks by night.

Ch 3 · Repetition and surprise

A night on the shift

The whole product is two moments: the handoff at night and the review in the morning. Everything in between is Shep's job. This is an example night, not real data.

Ch 3 · Habituation · Ch 12 · Kid A

The morning brief, with one surprise

The brief is the steady beat people come back for. The advanced features (opportunities, discovery, incidents) stop being menus. They show up now and then as one line in the brief, which is the contrast that keeps the routine from going stale. ASPM, Kubernetes and the full autonomous-SDLC vision are Shep's Kid A. Radiohead could release it only as a fourth album, after earlier albums had won the audience. Those features move behind shep labs.

Act III · Deep night

Getting heard

Ch 9 · The Audience of My Audience

Ask who is ready, not who is powerful

Bumble started by signing up an entire sorority, then used it as proof at the paired fraternity. Shep's first group should be solo founders who post their work in public. They are densely connected, they share constantly, and "my agents shipped 9 PRs last night" says something about who they are. Each group then becomes proof for the next.

Ch 4 · The Force of Story

Ch 8 · Find the pump

Plan the broadcasts. Don't wait for virality.

More than 90% of tweets never spread at all. Big hits come from a few moments that reach a million people at once, then a little sharing. Find what caused March, then set up two or three more like it in one named launch.

"Shep 2: Night Shift" launch week Proposed

Stop announcing near-daily releases. Bundle them into named launches. Show live usage on the front page ("PRs merged by Shep this week"). When people can see what others pick, demand concentrates on it.

Act IV · Dawn

Check the story before we tell it

Ch 5 · Vampire stories · Ch 7 · Compare with the failures

"Night shift" is a satisfying story, and the book warns that satisfying stories need checking first. Devin's "works while you sleep" pitch drew backlash for overpromising, and GitHub's Copilot agent already turns issues into PRs without you watching. Two tests come before any rebrand.

Do people actually leave it running alone?

Telemetry can show when runs start and how long they last.

If almost every run is interactive and during the day, the thesis is wrong. Find out before rebranding.

Who keeps the laptop awake?

Local-first means the machine has to stay on overnight.

A cloud night shift, for when the lid is closed, is the obvious paid tier. Local stays free.
